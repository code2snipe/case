# StudyOS

> a local ai study assistant built for students

StudyOS is a personal study assistant that runs locally on your computer.

The idea is simple:

instead of sending your notes, textbooks, and questions to some random cloud service, StudyOS keeps the whole system on your machine and uses local AI to help you study.

No API keys.
No cloud AI dependency.
No monthly AI subscription.

Just your study material + your computer + a local model.

---

## What StudyOS is

StudyOS is being built as a personal learning system rather than just another chatbot.

It is designed to understand the study material you give it and use that material when answering questions.
